they live in **Settings → General** and the **About** panel. These are
suggestions to apply there so the project is more discoverable and shares well.

## Social preview image

Settings → General → Social preview accepts a 1280×640 PNG. The `docs/hero.png`
capture (see `CAPTURE.md`) cropped/padded to that aspect ratio works well, or a
side-by-side of the Sod tube and a 2D Kelvin–Helmholtz frame.
